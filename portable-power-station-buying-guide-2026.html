What to check before checkout

Capacity: watt-hours tell you roughly how much energy is stored. Output: continuous watts must cover the devices you plan to run. Battery chemistry: LiFePO4 is common in newer models and is often chosen for cycle life. Ports: count the AC, USB-C and DC outputs you actually need. Recharge: compare wall, car and solar input speeds.
