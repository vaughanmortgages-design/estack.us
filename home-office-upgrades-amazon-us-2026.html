Spend where the friction is

If you finish the day with shoulder strain, screen height may matter more than buying a new desk. If the chair is the problem, measure your seat and desk relationship before spending. One targeted upgrade can outperform a full office makeover.

Before you buy

Measure the room, desk depth, available clamp space and the exact monitor or chair dimensions. Large-item returns can be inconvenient, so check the current seller and return terms before ordering.
