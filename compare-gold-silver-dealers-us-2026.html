Five things to compare

1. Total delivered cost: metal price, dealer premium, payment fees, shipping and insurance.

2. Product type: coins, rounds and bars can carry different premiums and resale considerations.

3. Payment method: card, bank transfer and other methods may have different costs or settlement times.

4. Delivery or storage: check insurance, signature requirements and any storage fees.

5. Exit path: understand the dealer's current buyback process before you purchase.
