The cost people miss

Subscriptions. A low purchase price can turn into a recurring cost if recordings, smart alerts or multi-camera history require a paid plan. Compare the total system cost over a year, not just the camera price.

Privacy and placement matter

Use cameras only where lawful and appropriate, avoid recording private spaces, and review account security, sharing controls and data-storage settings before installation.
