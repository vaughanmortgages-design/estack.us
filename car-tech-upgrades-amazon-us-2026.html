Quick answers

What this adapter does — and doesn’t do.

Will this add CarPlay to an older car that has no CarPlay?

No. These products are intended for vehicles that already have compatible wired CarPlay.

Why not just pick the cheapest one?

Compatibility and return terms matter more than a small price difference. Vehicle infotainment systems can behave differently by make, model and model year.
