What usually goes wrong

Compatibility mistakes: the product works, but not with the specific vehicle or phone setup. Cheap power hardware: weak cables, clamps or adapters create the failure point. Bad apps: hardware can be fine while setup and firmware are frustrating. Overbuying: extra features are useless if the core job is unreliable.

Quick rule

Buy for the one problem you want solved. Then compare the exact fit, required accessories and return policy before checkout.
